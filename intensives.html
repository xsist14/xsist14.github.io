Couples intensives

Step out of the weekly rhythm and create uninterrupted space for meaningful relationship work.

Deep work, dedicated time

Make room for change without watching the clock.

Longer sessions allow you to slow down, focus on specific issues, learn practical tools, and work through conflict more efficiently.

01

Half-day intensives

Four focused hours

Half-day couples intensives offer a powerful opportunity to transform your relationship in a concentrated session. They may be especially helpful when:

  • You are experiencing significant distress
  • You desire quicker, focused results
  • Weekly scheduling is difficult
  • You want to kickstart positive change

By dedicating an uninterrupted block of time, you can dive deep into specific issues, learn communication tools, and begin strengthening your bond.

02

Two-hour sessions

Follow-up and integration

Two-hour sessions are particularly useful after a half-day intensive. They give you space to revisit the tools and strategies you learned and integrate them into daily life.

We’ll review your progress, fine-tune your approach, and address new challenges. The longer format can also allow sessions to be spaced every two to four weeks.

Focused time can help you interrupt old patterns and begin practicing a different way of being together.
