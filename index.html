The heart of the work

Facing stress with compassion instead of avoidance.

In this fast-paced world, we can react to or avoid our stresses rather than face them, understand them, shift our views of them, and act according to our experiences. Therapy creates room to slow down and find more peace of mind.

Through compassionate exploration and guidance, we create a safe space to honor and understand different aspects of yourself, fostering acceptance and integration.

Park-based therapy

A calmer setting for meaningful conversation.

Daniel meets with people in Chicago parks for therapy that can include walking, sitting, reflecting, and reconnecting with a sense of presence. Being outdoors can support a gentler rhythm and a more grounded way of talking through difficult things.

Daniel Gaytan · he/him

Down to earth, good natured, empathetic, and friendly.

I am a Chicagoan who is Mexican American, with a deep connection to family and friends, and nearly twenty years of experience working with people in the mental health field.

It is my belief that each human has the capacity for transformation and becoming their most authentic self.

By acknowledging and embracing challenging parts of ourselves, we can work toward healing past wounds, resolving inner conflicts, and reclaiming selfhood.

“The point of therapy is caring, supporting, and empowering.”

The practice can bring more peace of mind about who we are, opening and freeing our possibilities and potential so we can connect with our good nature and be present.
