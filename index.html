About

A safe, supportive space for healing and self-understanding.

I am a down-to-earth, empathetic, and compassionate therapist with nearly twenty years of experience supporting people in the mental health field. My work is rooted in creating a warm and supportive environment where healing, acceptance, and transformation can unfold naturally.

Through compassionate exploration and guidance, we work together to better understand difficult emotions, heal past wounds, and strengthen your connection to yourself and others.
